Open **Settings** from the account menu at the bottom of the dashboard sidebar. Everything on this page is under **Account**, the first button at the top right of Settings.

## Account

- **Profile photo**: upload, replace or remove it. PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, up to 1 MB. Your teammates see it on the Team page, on the folders you share and in the sharing window.
- **Display name**: up to 80 characters. Click **Save changes**.
- **Email**: click **Change email address**, type the new one and click **Send verification email**. Open the email and confirm; the dashboard updates by itself.
- **Team** and **Member since** show your team, your role in it, and when you joined. **Manage** opens the [Team page](/team/workspace).

Changes reach the extension and your phone automatically.

## Company branding

Add your company's logo and name. They appear next to the Fibonax name at the top of the dashboard.

- **Logo**: PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG, up to 1 MB. It also becomes the icon in your browser tab on the dashboard and the Fibonax button in your Chrome toolbar.
- **Company name**: shown when there's no logo, and as the logo's hover text.

The logo changes as soon as you pick it. The name needs **Save changes**.

## Unused snippets and prompts

Choose how long a snippet or prompt can go unused before a notice appears on the Snippets and Prompts pages: 6, 7, 8 or 9 months. It counts from the last time it was inserted, or from the day it was added if it was never used.

## Monthly recap email

On the morning of the 1st, Fibonax emails you one page about the month just ended, with the same figures as your [Analytics](/settings/analytics) page: snippets inserted and how that compares with the month before, time saved, active days, your longest streak, different snippets used, prompts used, fields filled in, your busiest day, a calendar of the month with darker days for more inserts, and the few snippets that did most of your work. At the top, a line marks a milestone when you pass one (1,000 snippets inserted since you started, for example) or your busiest month so far.

You get it only for a month in which you used Fibonax. A month with no inserts sends nothing.

- **Email me a recap on the 1st of each month**: the switch under **Account**. It is on for every account until turned off, and it is part of your account, not of one device.
- **Email me last month's recap**: sends last month's recap to your address right away, so you can see it with your own figures. It works whether the switch is on or off.
- **Stop the monthly recap**: the link at the bottom of every recap email. One click turns the switch off, without signing in. To turn it back on, use the switch here.

The email comes from `ciao@fibonax.com`. Days are counted in Rome time, the product's home time.

## Santo del Giorno

The footer at the bottom of the dashboard shows the saint of the day, read from Vatican News, like this: `Santo del Giorno · San Luca`. It is on by default. To hide it, switch off **Santo del Giorno** under **Account**. The switch applies to this device only, and the extension popup has its own.

If Fibonax cannot read the saint, for example when you are offline, the footer leaves it out until it can.

Next to it, the footer always shows how many days are left until Christmas. That line has no switch.

The open sidebar also carries a short motivational sentence above your name. It changes every 4 days and has no switch either. On a very short window it steps aside until there is room for it.

## FiboToken exchange rate

The **FiboToken exchange rate** card shows what one FiboToken is worth in euros today, marked **Variable rate**, with an example of how many FiboTokens an amount buys and the day the rate was set. **Rate details** opens the full explanation: how a change works, and that a new rate applies to new packs only. FiboTokens you already own keep their value: one FiboToken, one use.

**See the packs** opens the [pricing page](https://fibonax.com/pricing/). Until packs go on sale, the window also says the day they start.

The same rate shows in the extension popup's settings (see [Install the extension](/install-extension)) and at the foot of Home on your phone (see [Mobile](/apps/mobile)).

## Triggers

The **Triggers & Prefix** card sets what you type to insert snippets and prompts. See [Triggers](/settings/triggers).

## Delete account

The last card under **Account** deletes your account. It cannot be undone.

1. Click **Delete account**.
2. Read what will happen. If you're the only admin of a team, the window names that team: it is deleted for everyone in it. To keep it, make someone else an admin on the [Team page](/team/workspace) first.
3. Type `DELETE` and click **Delete account**.

What happens:

- Your personal snippets, prompts, folders, labels and Brains are deleted, with your photo, logo and uploaded files.
- What you added to a team stays with the team. Another admin of that team takes it over.
- You're signed out on every device, including the extension and your phone.

You can delete your account only here in the dashboard, not from the extension or the phone. On a phone, use a computer: the dashboard opens on screens 1024 pixels wide or more.
