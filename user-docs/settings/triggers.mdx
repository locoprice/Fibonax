A trigger is the short sequence that tells Fibonax you want a snippet or a prompt:

| For | Default | You type |
|---|---|---|
| Snippets | `::` | `::quote` |
| Prompts | `"""` | `"""followup` |

## Change a trigger

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the dashboard, open **Settings**. The **Triggers & Prefix** card is on the **Account** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new sequence">
    Change the **Snippet** or **Prompt** box. It saves when you click away, and shows **Saved**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A trigger must be:

- 1 to 5 characters, with no spaces.
- Not a single letter or number, so it can't fire while you write normal text.
- Different from the other trigger, and not the start of it. `::` and `::x` can't both be used.

The extension and your phone pick up the change on their own.

## Insert automatically

Type your trigger and a few letters, and the **Insert snippet** menu opens. By default you choose from it with <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Turn on **Insert automatically** and Fibonax picks for you when only one snippet fits.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    In the dashboard, open **Settings**. The **Insert automatically** card is on the **Account** tab, under **Triggers & Prefix**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    Switch on **Insert when only one snippet fits**. It saves as soon as you click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the wait">
    Under **Wait before inserting**, pick from 1 to 10 seconds. The default is 2 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then, with at least three letters typed after the trigger and only one snippet left in the menu, the menu shows **Inserting in 2 seconds** and inserts the snippet when the time is up. For example, if `::quote` is your only trigger starting with "quo", typing `::quo` inserts it.

While you wait:

- The menu stays open, so you can read it.
- <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> inserts right away.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd>, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> or an arrow key cancels. Another letter starts the wait again.

It is off by default. A trigger you type in full, like `::quote`, still inserts when you pause. The extension picks up the change on its own.

## Extension options

Three more options live in the extension itself: click the Fibonax icon in Chrome, then the gear.

- **Suggest on selection**: offer matching snippets when you select text in a field. On by default.
- **Automatic capitalization**: start a snippet with a capital letter at the start of a sentence. On by default.
- **Default Language**: the language picked first when a snippet has several.

These belong to that browser only.
