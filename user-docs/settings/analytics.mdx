Open **Analytics** in the dashboard sidebar. Each time a snippet is inserted, the extension records it, and this page counts those inserts.

## Summary cards

- **Total snippets**: how many snippets you own.
- **Triggers this week**: snippets inserted in the last seven days.
- **Time saved**: an estimate, counting 30 seconds for each insert.
- **Top trigger**: your most-inserted snippet recently, and how many times.

## Activity

A grid of the last twelve months, one square per day. The darker the square, the more you inserted that day.

Under the grid, one row of figures shows how many snippets, prompts, folders and Brains you have, your inserts this year, and your current and longest streak of days with at least one insert. **Contribution activity** then shows this month in one short line: how many times you inserted snippets and the folders you used. Select **Show earlier months** to see the months before it, and **Show less** to fold them away again.

Two switches in the activity settings control what you see:

- **Private contributions**: show the exact counts in the heading and when you point at a square.
- **Activity overview**: show or hide the breakdown under the grid.

## Recent use

- **Usage** chart: inserts per day over the last two weeks.
- **Top triggers**: your five most-inserted snippets over the same two weeks, each with a small trend line.
