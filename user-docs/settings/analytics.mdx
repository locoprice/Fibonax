Open **Analytics** in the dashboard sidebar, or tap **More**, then **Analytics** on your phone. Each time a snippet is inserted, the extension or the phone records it, and this page counts those inserts. The dashboard and the phone show the same figures.

## Pick a period

At the top, choose **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **12 months**. Each period ends today. Every figure is compared with the period just before it, of the same length, and the dates of both periods are shown under the title. Your choice is remembered next time.

Next to each figure, a small label says how it moved: **↑ +12%**, **↓ −3%**, **New** when there was nothing before, or **No change**.

Press **Refresh** (the circular arrow) to read the latest inserts.

## Summary

- **Snippets inserted**: how many times you inserted a snippet, and the average per day.
- **Time saved**: an estimate, counting 30 seconds for each insert.
- **Active days**: how many days you used Fibonax, and how many inserts on those days.
- **Different snippets used**: how many of your snippets you used at least once.

Under them: your **busiest day**, your **daily streak** (days in a row with at least one insert; it carries on until the end of the day after your last insert), **fields filled per insert**, and the share of inserts from Monday to Friday.

## Inserts per day

A bar for each day of the period. The dashed line is the same day in the period before, and from 30 days up, a dark line shows the average of the last seven days.

## Top snippets and movers

**Top snippets** lists your ten most-inserted snippets, each with its share of all your inserts and how it moved. Inserts of a snippet you have since deleted are counted as **Deleted snippet**. On your phone, tap a snippet to open it.

**Rising fastest** and **Falling fastest** list the snippets whose use went up or down the most compared with the period before.

## When you use snippets

A grid of the week, Monday to Sunday, by hour of the day in your local time. The darker the square, the more you inserted at that hour. Under it: your busiest weekday and hour.

## Where your use goes

Your inserts by **folder** and by **language**, each with its share and how it moved.

## Team

If you belong to a team, this table shows who uses the shared snippets: each person's inserts, how many different snippets they used, their share and how it moved, then the whole team. It counts only snippets in team folders you can open, and shows one total per person, never single inserts or times.

## Library health

How many of the snippets you can use (yours and shared with you) were used in this period, have a shortcut, and sit in a folder.

- **Never used**: snippets nobody has ever inserted, oldest first.
- **Not used for 90 days**: snippets whose last insert, by you or your team, was more than 90 days ago.

## Activity

On the dashboard, a grid of the last twelve months, one square per day. Under it, an overview shows how many snippets, prompts and folders you have, which folders you use most, and a month-by-month breakdown.

Two switches in **Contribution settings** control what you see:

- **Private contributions**: show the exact counts in the heading and when you point at a square.
- **Activity overview**: show or hide the breakdown under the grid.
