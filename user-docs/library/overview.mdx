The starter library holds ready-made snippets, prompts and Brains. Pick the ones you want and add them in one click. Once added, they are yours: edit, rename, move or delete them like anything you made yourself.

## Add from the library

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the library">
    On the Snippets, Prompts or Brains page, click **Browse library** next to the New button. An empty page offers it too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look before you add">
    Click a topic to narrow the list. Point at an item to see it on the right: a snippet shows the boxes it asks for and how it reads with an example filled in, a prompt shows what it tells the AI, a Brain shows the items it holds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick and add">
    Tick what you want, or **Select all**, then click **Add**. A message confirms how many were added.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where they go

- **Snippets and prompts** go into a folder called **From the library**, made the first time you add something. Move them anywhere afterwards.
- **Brains** are added as new Brains, each with items to fill in. Replace the "Write here" text with your own facts.

## Good to know

- **Already there.** An item with the same name and the same text as one you have is skipped, and the message says how many.
- **Shortcut taken.** If you already use a shortcut such as `::thanks`, the new snippet gets `::thanks_2`. Change it in the editor if you prefer another.
- **Currency.** Prices in the sales snippets print in EUR. Change the word in the body to your own currency.
- **A Brain behind a prompt.** **Reply to a customer** and **Handle a complaint** work best with your facts attached: open the prompt, and in the Context block choose a Brain in the **Brain** box. See [Use a Brain as context](/prompts/create-prompts#use-a-brain-as-context).
- **On your phone.** The library opens on the dashboard. What you add there shows up on the phone as usual.
- **From the extension.** When a list in the extension popup is empty, **Browse the starter library** opens the dashboard with the library ready.
