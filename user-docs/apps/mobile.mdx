Open [app.fibonax.com/mobile](https://app.fibonax.com/mobile/) in your phone's browser and sign in. Opening app.fibonax.com on a phone takes you there too.

The phone page reads the same library as the dashboard and the extension. What you change on the computer shows up here.

Home asks **What's next?** and shows three buttons: **Use a snippet** opens your snippets, **Prepare a prompt** opens your prompts and **Capture a note** opens Save to Brain. Each button sinks a little when you tap it. Tap your profile picture at the top for your profile. If you have no picture, it shows your initials. The app version and the month of the last update sit centred under the buttons.

Under **Ready to reuse**, Home shows your first pinned snippet and pinned prompt as cards, with how many times each has been used. Tap a card, or **Fill & copy**, **Copy text** or **Copy prompt** under it, to use it. Tap **⋯** for the rest of its menu. Below the cards, **Search snippets** opens the snippet search, and **Your Brains** opens your Brains.

The bottom bar opens **Snippets**, **Prompts**, **Home** and **Brains**. Tap **More** for search, Analytics or Profile.

Snippets, Prompts and Brains open with the same dark header as Home: your greeting and profile picture, the page's name with how many it holds, the search box and **Filters** beside it. Tap **Filters** to open the choices under the search box; the number on the button shows how many filters are on, and **Clear all** turns them off. Under the header, three tabs switch between **Prompts**, **Snippets** and **Brains**: the page you're on is the blue one.

At the bottom of every page you see your company logo, or your company name if you have no logo. You choose the logo and name in the dashboard. See [Account and branding](/settings/account). The phone checks for a new logo or name each time you open the page, so you don't need to sign in again to see a change.

## Find a snippet

- Search from the box at the top by title, shortcut, keyword, label or body text.
- Tap **Details** beside a snippet to read all its labels, search keywords and available languages in separate sections. Languages appear by name inside this panel. **Updated** names the language the date applies to, or **All languages** when translations share the same saved update. Long values wrap in the panel. Use **Close details**, tap outside or swipe down to return to the list.
- Tap **Filters** beside the search box to narrow the list by folder, language and label.
- Snippets load 25 at a time. Scroll down and tap **Load more** for the rest.
- The number on a snippet's icon shows how many times it has been used. A snippet in a shared folder shows the team's total; a personal snippet shows your total.
- A badge next to the name shows a snippet that is not approved yet, or is deprecated. A deprecated snippet still works: it shows a warning when you open it and when you copy it. Archived snippets, prompts and Brain items don't appear on the phone. See [Review and approval](/review/overview).

## Use a snippet

Tap **⋯** on a snippet:

- **Use now**: copies the snippet. If it has fields or several languages, it opens the snippet first, so you can pick the language and fill in the form, then tap **Copy** or **Share**.
- **Copy text**: copies it as it is.
- **Pin to top**: keeps it at the top of your list.
- **Share…**: sends the text through your phone's Share menu. If the snippet has fields, it opens the snippet first, so you can fill them in, then tap **Share**.
- **Snippet info**: shows its details.

Use **Fill in** to answer the fields and **Preview** to read the complete message. Long forms have sections you can collapse, in the snippet's original order. Open a choice menu to select its answers; date and time settings are under **Options**. What you type shows in **bold**; the copied or shared text is plain.

Empty optional labelled lines are omitted. Fields needed by calculations are checked before **Copy** or **Share**; a missing value brings you back to that field. Zero remains a valid answer. See [Empty fields and calculations](/text-snippets/insert#empty-fields-and-calculations).

If the form starts with **Fill from link**, paste a link to a web page there and tap **Fill**: the fields fill in from what the page says, each marked **From link**. Check them, then tap **Copy** or **Share**. See [Fill a form from a link](/text-snippets/fill-from-link).

Before a snippet is copied or shared, the phone checks the text for anything that looks unfinished, such as `[xxx]`, `XXX` or a box left empty, as the extension does. **This text may not be finished** lists what it found. **Fix it** takes you to the box in question; **Copy anyway** or **Share anyway** goes ahead. Snippets aren't edited on the phone, so a gap written in the snippet itself has no **Fix it** here: copy it anyway and fix it in your message, or change the snippet on a computer. **Don't warn me about this snippet again** stops the warning for that snippet everywhere. See [Check before it goes out](/text-snippets/insert#check-before-it-goes-out).

### Share instead of paste

Under a snippet you've opened, **Share** sits next to **Copy**. It opens your phone's Share menu with the finished text: pick WhatsApp, email, Messages or any other app, then the chat or contact, and the text is already in the message box. Nothing to paste.

**Copy** is still there for apps that aren't in the Share menu: tap it, switch to the app, and paste. If your phone's browser has no Share menu, you only see **Copy**.

## Prompts

Tap **Prompts** in the bottom bar to switch to your prompt library. The number on a prompt's icon shows how many times it has been used; prompts in a shared folder show the team's total. Labels are searchable here too; tap **Details** on a labelled prompt to read them. Each prompt has the same menu, with **Prompt info** instead of Snippet info. A prompt has nothing to fill in, so **Share…** sends it straight away through your phone's Share menu.

If **Interactive Steps** is switched on in this browser, copied prompts start with the step-by-step instructions. See [Write a prompt](/prompts/create-prompts#interactive-steps).

A prompt that uses a [Brain](/prompts/create-prompts#use-a-brain-as-context) copies and shares with everything in that Brain, as it is now: the page reads the Brain again each time you come back to it. **Prompt info** names the Brain. If the Brain is still loading when you tap, the page says so: tap again in a moment. A Brain that is not yours, is in the trash or could not be loaded is named in the text as not available, and the message after copying says so.

## Brains

Tap **Brains** in the bottom bar to see your [Brains](/brains/overview). Tap one to open it and see what's inside, then tap an item to read all of it, then **Copy** it or **Share** it to another app.

- **Copy whole Brain**: copies every item in the Brain as one block, ready to paste into ChatGPT, Claude or another AI app, in the same form the **Context** button uses on a computer. If the Brain is long, the page says so: some AI apps cut a long paste, so check what arrived.
- **Add text**: adds a new item to the Brain. Give it a name and a body; the summary is optional. It saves to the same library the dashboard shows.
- **Paste & save**: saves what you copied, text or a link, into the Brain. See [Save from other apps](#save-from-other-apps).
- Search from the box at the top. From the list of Brains it looks inside every Brain; inside a Brain, only in that one.
- Tap **Filters** beside the search box to show one kind of item: facts, notes, documents or conversations.

Tap **⋯** on an item:

- **Use now**: copies it ready for an AI chat, with its name, the way the Context button adds it.
- **Copy text**: copies only its text.
- **Share…**: sends the text through your phone's Share menu.
- **Item info**: shows its Brain, kind, size and dates.

Tap **⋯** on a Brain to **Open** it, **Copy whole Brain**, **Share…** it, **Add text** or **Paste & save** to it, or see **Brain info**.

## Save from other apps

Found something worth keeping while you browse: a paragraph, a link, an answer? Send it to a Brain without retyping it. Each way below opens **Save to Brain** with the text: pick the Brain, change the text if you like, and tap **Save**.

Fibonax picks the Brain for you: the one whose name, description and items share the most words with what you're saving. The line under the Brain list says why, for example *Suggested because it mentions pasta and recipe*. If two Brains fit equally, it names both; if none fits, it says **No Brain fits this yet** and keeps the Brain you used last. Pick another Brain whenever you like: Fibonax leaves your choice alone. Everything happens on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere to decide.

It goes by words only, so a new, empty Brain is found only through its name and description. Give each Brain a description with the words its items will use, for example *Recipes, pasta, ricette*, and it is suggested from the first save.

The item is saved as a note, named after the page, or after its first words, followed by the date and time. A shared link stays in the text, so you can find the page again. Very long text is saved as several items, numbered in order.

### Paste & save, on any phone

1. Copy the text or the link in any app.
2. Open Fibonax, tap **Brains**, then **Paste & save**. Inside a Brain, it saves to that Brain.
3. Check the Brain and tap **Save**.

If your phone asks whether Fibonax may paste, allow it. If the text doesn't appear, press and hold the empty box and tap **Paste**.

### Text in a screenshot or a photo

Your phone can copy the text out of a picture for you, then Paste & save does the rest:

- **iPhone:** open the picture in Photos, press and hold the text (Live Text), and tap **Copy**.
- **Android:** open the picture in Google Photos, tap **Lens**, select the text and tap **Copy text**.

Then open Fibonax, tap **Brains**, then **Paste & save**.

### From the Share menu, on Android

Add Fibonax to your phone once:

1. Open [app.fibonax.com/mobile](https://app.fibonax.com/mobile/) in Chrome and sign in.
2. Tap **⋮**, then **Add to Home screen** and **Install**. Some phones call it **Install app**.

From then on, **Fibonax** is listed when you tap **Share** in Chrome or in any other app. Pick it, and Save to Brain opens with what you shared.

### With a Shortcut, on iPhone

An iPhone doesn't list web apps in its Share menu, so a Shortcut takes their place. Set it up once in the **Shortcuts** app:

1. Tap **+** to start a new shortcut.
2. Add the action **URL Encode**. It encodes the **Shortcut Input**.
3. Add the action **Open URLs**. In its box, type `https://app.fibonax.com/mobile/?text=` and, right after it, insert the variable **URL Encoded Text**.
4. Rename the shortcut **Save to Fibonax**.
5. Open the shortcut's details (**ⓘ**) and turn on **Show in Share Sheet**.

Now tap **Share** in Safari or in any app and pick **Save to Fibonax**. Fibonax opens in Safari with what you shared; the first time, sign in there.

## Other tabs

Tap **More** in the bottom bar to open **Search this section**, **Analytics** or **Profile**. Search moves focus to the search box for the page you're viewing. **Profile** shows the account you're signed in with, a link to this documentation, and **Sign out**.

<Note>
  The phone page is for using your library, not managing it. Create and edit snippets, prompts and folders, create, rename or delete Brains, edit items and upload files in the dashboard on a computer.
</Note>
