<Note>
  FiboChat is part of Fibonax Pro. Without Pro, it shows a lock and **Available to Pro users soon**.
</Note>

FiboChat answers questions from what your team has already written in Fibonax. You don't need to remember a shortcut, a folder or the exact words of a snippet: type the question the way you would ask a colleague.

It answers only from your own snippets and Brain items. It never makes up a rule, a price, a deadline or a procedure that isn't written there.

## Ask a question

### In the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the search window">
    Click the search bar at the top, or press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows) from any page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to FiboChat">
    Click the sparkle button beside the search box. Type your question, three characters or more, then press **Enter** or click **Ask**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the answer">
    **Back to results** returns to the normal search list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### On your phone

Tap the search box on the Snippets, Prompts or Brains page, then tap the sparkle button beside the box that opens. Type your question and tap **Ask**. The answer opens in a sheet at the bottom of the screen.

## Read the answer

- **From your Fibonax** marks an answer built from your content.
- The small numbers after a sentence show where it came from. Click or tap one to open that snippet or Brain.
- **Sources** lists every item the answer was built from, with when it was last updated. Items in grey were found but not used.
- **Not in Fibonax** names the part of your question your content doesn't cover yet.
- **Sources disagree** appears when two items say different things. Check both and fix the one that's out of date.
- **Copy answer** copies the answer without the source numbers, ready to paste into a message. For a pasted message it reads **Copy reply**.

When nothing in your Fibonax covers the question, it says **Nothing in Fibonax covers this yet** instead of answering. When it finds related items that don't answer the question, it says so and lists them.

## Reply to a message

Got an email or a chat that asks several things at once? Paste the whole message where you would type a question, up to 4,000 characters, and open **FiboChat**. It finds every question in the message, even ones written without a question mark, like "let me know if…", and writes one reply that answers each of them in order.

- The **questions found** list shows each question with how far your Fibonax answers it: **Answered**, **Partly answered** or **Not in Fibonax**. The small numbers after it open the items that answer it.
- **Reply** is ready to send: a greeting, every question answered in the order it was asked, and a short closing, in the language of the message.
- A question your Fibonax can't answer is never guessed. It shows in the reply as a note in brackets, highlighted, saying what to add, for example **[Add: whether parking is available]**.
- **Copy reply** copies the reply without the source numbers. If a note in brackets is still there, Fibonax reminds you to fill it in before you send.

To make the next reply complete, save the missing answer as a snippet or a Brain item.

## What it reads

- Your snippets, and snippets in folders shared with you.
- Your Brain items.

It reads only what you can already open. Prompts are not used as sources: a prompt is an instruction for an AI, not information about your work.

It prefers approved items, says so when an item it used is not approved yet, treats a deprecated item as out of date, and ignores archived items. See [Review and approval](/review/overview).

To answer, Fibonax sends your question, or the message you pasted, and the matching items to Claude, the AI model made by Anthropic. Nothing else from your library is sent.

Each person can use AI features 30 times an hour and 200 times a day, Ask and Draft with AI together. Past that, Fibonax says so; try again later. A question that finds nothing in your Fibonax doesn't count.

## Tell us if an answer was wrong

Under every answer, **Was this right?** offers six choices: **Useful**, **Wrong**, **Outdated**, **Incomplete**, **Not relevant** and **Conflicts with policy**. Pick one; it is sent once.

Your choice goes to a review list. It never changes a snippet or a Brain by itself: a person reads it and fixes the source.

## Review feedback

Click **Review** in the sidebar, then **Answer feedback**.

- **Open** lists feedback still waiting for someone. Each row shows the choice, who sent it and when, the question, the answer, and the items it was built from. Click an item to open it and fix it.
- **Mark resolved** closes the row once the source is fixed. **Resolved** lists closed rows, and **Reopen** brings one back.

Team admins see the feedback their whole team sends. Everyone else sees their own.
