## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it to Chrome">
    Open [Fibonax on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/khdpimdpkgmmaimpbfjgglnpaemmopoo), click **Add to Chrome**, then **Add extension**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin it">
    Click the puzzle icon in the Chrome toolbar and the pin next to Fibonax, so its icon stays in view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click the Fibonax icon and choose **Sign in via dashboard**. A tab opens on app.fibonax.com, links the extension to your account and closes by itself.

    Prefer a code? Choose **Use email code instead**, type your email and click **Send code**, then type the code from the email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The extension needs Chrome on a computer, or another browser that installs extensions from the Chrome Web Store.

## What it adds

- **Snippets as you type**: type `::` and a trigger in any text field. See [Ways to insert](/text-snippets/insert).
- **Prompts as you type**: type `"""` for your prompt menu. See [Prompts](/prompts/overview).
- **A right-click menu**: **Insert Fibonax snippet** in text fields, and **Save selection to Fibonax** on selected text.
- **A Context button on AI chats**: add what your [Brains](/brains/overview) know to a message in ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants. The shortcut is <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> on a Mac).

## The popup

Click the Fibonax icon in the toolbar:

- Switch between **Snippets**, **Prompts** and **Brains**, and search. Snippets also filter by folder.
- Click a snippet to open it, fill in its fields and click **Copy filled**.
- On **Brains**, click a Brain to see what is in it. **Copy whole Brain** copies all of it, ready to paste into an AI chat. Click an item to copy just that item. See [Use a Brain in an AI chat](/brains/overview#use-a-brain-in-an-ai-chat).
- **Open Dashboard** takes you to app.fibonax.com.
- The footer shows the saint of the day, like `Santo del Giorno · San Luca`, and how many days are left until Christmas. See [Account and branding](/settings/account).
- On a ChatGPT or Claude conversation, **Save this chat** keeps it in a Brain.

The gear opens the extension's own settings: **Automatic capitalization**, **Santo del Giorno** (the saint in the footer), **Default Language**, **Sign out** and **Delete account**, which opens [your account settings](/settings/account#delete-account) in the dashboard. Snippets, folders and triggers are managed in the dashboard.

## Asking for a review

On a free account, Fibonax asks for a short Chrome Web Store review. From your tenth sign-in, a line saying you are using an enterprise tool for free, with a **Leave us a review** button, appears at the top of the dashboard and in the popup. It shows once per sign-in, at most five times.

Click **Leave us a review** or **Don't ask again** and it stops for good, on every device you use.

## Updates

Chrome updates the extension by itself. To get a new version straight away, open `chrome://extensions`, switch on **Developer mode** at the top right and click **Update**.

SprintBrain is now Fibonax. If you had the extension before, the popup says so once after the update. Your account, snippets and prompts stay as they were.
