## Type the trigger

Type your snippet prefix and the trigger, like `::quote`. Fibonax inserts the snippet when you pause, or straight away when you press <kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

- **A few letters are enough, if you want.** Turn on **Insert automatically** in [Triggers](/settings/triggers) and `::quo` inserts the snippet after a short wait, when no other trigger starts with "quo". The menu stays open while you wait. Off by default.
- **Capitals don't matter.** `::Quote` works the same as `::quote`.
- **Alternative queries work too.** The extra words saved on a snippet act as triggers, so `::estimate` can insert the same snippet as `::quote`.

## Pick from the menu

Type the prefix on its own (`::`) and wait a moment. The **Insert snippet** menu opens at the cursor.

- Keep typing to narrow the list.
- <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> move through it, <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> inserts, <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.
- <kbd>Space</kbd> closes the menu and keeps what you typed.

Prompts have a menu of their own: type `"""`. See [Prompts](/prompts/overview).

## Fill in the form

When a snippet has fields, a form opens where you typed. Fill in each box, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to insert or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel.

The form opens in a wide window with the snippet name at the top. Fields stay in the order of the snippet. Long forms have sections that start open and can be collapsed. On wider screens, the full **Message preview** stays beside the fields, with separate scrolling. On smaller screens, switch between **Fill in** and **Preview**. Choose **Insert message** when ready; the button stays in view.

Each field has a label above it. What you type shows in **bold**; the inserted text stays plain. Open a choice menu to pick an answer. Multiple-choice menus still accept several answers, and **Other** opens a box for your own answer. Date and time settings are under **Options**.

Date boxes open on today. You can change how a date prints, jump to a day such as **Tomorrow** or **Next Monday**, or set a time. These choices apply to this insert only. The saved snippet doesn't change.

Some forms start with **Fill from link**. Paste a link to a web page, such as an order or a confirmation, and press **Fill** or <kbd>Enter</kbd>: the fields fill in from what the page says, each marked **From link**. Check them, then press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to insert. See [Fill a form from a link](/text-snippets/fill-from-link).

### Empty fields and calculations

An unanswered optional field on its own labelled line, such as `Request: {REQUEST}`, removes that line, including the label. Spaces alone count as empty. **0 is a value**, so it stays in the message. Lines with another answered field, formulas, rules or longer independent prose are kept; use a **Show or hide** rule when you need to control a larger block.

Fields needed by an active calculation have an asterisk. Before inserting or copying, Fibonax points you to any missing value, invalid number, date before its allowed start, or unanswered **Other** choice. Complete that field and try again. Calculations in a hidden conditional branch do not require an answer. The preview shows an ellipsis for calculations still waiting for input.

These rules apply in the overlay, extension popup, editor preview and mobile. They do not change the saved snippet or its defaults. **Copy raw** continues to copy the original template.

## Choose a language

If a snippet has more than one language, Fibonax asks you to **Choose a language to insert**. To have one language picked for you each time, set **Default Language** in the extension popup's settings.

## Expansion summary

The summary shows the word and character counts for this expansion, plus an estimate based on typing 3.3 characters per second. The time is an estimate, not a measurement of your personal typing speed.

- **Text inserted** means the text is already in the field. Choose **Done** to keep it or **Undo insertion** to take it back out. Closing the card or pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd> keeps the text.
- **Ready to insert** means the text is waiting for confirmation. Choose **Insert text** to continue or **Cancel** to stop. Closing the card or pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels the insertion.

If the snippet is deprecated, the card says so instead of its usual line: *This snippet is deprecated. Check it is still right before you send it.* Archived snippets don't expand at all. See [Review and approval](/review/overview).

The card shows whether it will close or insert automatically after five seconds. The progress bar shows the time remaining. Moving the pointer onto the card or using its keyboard controls pauses that countdown and keeps the bar visible, so you have time to review. After you choose an action, focus returns to the text field. You can also click back into the text field and keep typing while the card is open: what you type goes into your message, and the card still closes on its own.

A small confetti burst plays once around the status icon when the summary opens. It stays inside the card and does not cover the controls. Deprecated snippets do not show confetti. With reduced motion enabled on your device, there is no confetti; the countdown bar remains visible and advances in one-second steps.

## Undo

Choose **Undo insertion** while the **Text inserted** card is open to remove the inserted text. If the card says **Ready to insert**, choose **Cancel** instead.

## Right-click menu

Right-click inside a text field and open **Insert Fibonax snippet**:

- **Recent**: the last five snippets you used.
- Your folders, with their subfolders inside them.
- **Unfiled**: snippets that aren't in a folder.

A snippet with several languages opens a submenu with one entry per language.

## Copy from the popup

Click the Fibonax icon in the Chrome toolbar and search. Click a snippet to open it, fill in its fields and click **Copy filled**, then paste the text where you need it. If the form starts with **Fill from link**, you can paste a page's link there to fill the fields first.

- **Copy raw** copies the body with its fields still in it.
- **Copy shortcut** copies the trigger.

Use the popup for fields that don't accept typed expansion.

## On your phone

Open [app.fibonax.com/mobile](https://app.fibonax.com/mobile/) to search, fill and copy snippets. See [Mobile](/apps/mobile).

## Capital letters

With **Automatic capitalization** on (the default), a snippet inserted at the start of a message, on a new line, or after `.` `!` `?` starts with a capital letter. In the middle of a sentence it keeps the capitals you wrote. You can turn it off in the extension popup's settings.
