You share folders, not single snippets. Everyone you share a folder with can use everything inside it: its snippets and its prompts. Anything you add to the folder later is shared too.

Brains stay personal. A shared prompt that [uses one of your Brains](/prompts/create-prompts#use-a-brain-as-context) goes to your teammates with a line saying the Brain is not available, never with what is in it.

## Share a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder's menu">
    On the Snippets page, right-click the folder in the rail and choose **Share with team…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who">
    Pick **Whole team** or a teammate. Use the search box to find people by name or email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the access level">
    See the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Share">
    It's in their extension and dashboard straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Access level | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Can use** | Use everything in the folder. |
| **Can edit** | Use and edit the items inside. |
| **Full control** | Everything, including sharing. Only for single teammates, not the whole team. |

**People with access** lists everyone who has the folder, who shared it and when. The folder owner and team admins can change a level there, or remove someone with the bin icon.

## See who used it last

Every snippet and prompt in a team folder says who used it last, and when:

> Last used by Anna on 30/10/2026 at 14:05

If nobody on the team has used it yet, it says **Never used**. A use is any time someone inserts it in a page, copies or shares it from the extension or the phone, or clicks **Use** on a prompt in the dashboard.

Where to find it:

- **Dashboard**: at the bottom of the snippet editor and the prompt editor, and at the bottom of each prompt card.
- **Extension**: at the bottom of a snippet's detail, and under each prompt in the Prompts tab.
- **Phone**: at the bottom of the info sheet of a snippet or prompt.

Your own uses show straight away. A teammate's use shows the next time you open the page, or when you come back to it.

To see who uses the team's snippets and prompts most, open [Analytics](/settings/analytics#team).

## Good to know

- **Sharing needs a team.** If you don't have one yet, the share window lets you create it on the spot.
- **A folder belongs to one team.** Once shared with a team, it stays with that team.
- **Only the folder's owner or a team admin can change its sharing.** Everyone else sees who has access.
- **Adding to a team folder needs Can edit.** To create a snippet or prompt in a team folder, or move one into it, you need **Can edit** or **Full control** on that folder.

See [Your team](/team/workspace) to invite people.
