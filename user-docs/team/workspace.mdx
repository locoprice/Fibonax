Open **Team** in the dashboard sidebar. The number next to it counts shared folders: the ones you share and the ones shared with you. Hover over it to see it written out.

## Create a team

If you don't have a team yet, the Team page asks for a name, like `Acme Ltd`. Click **Create team**. You can rename it later.

Were you invited instead? Click **Check your invitations** on the same page.

## Invite people

Admins and managers see an **Invite people** button on the People list. Click it:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add email addresses">
    Type or paste them. Commas, spaces and line breaks all separate addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Join as">
    See the roles below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send invite**. Each person gets an email and decides whether to join. Nothing is shared with them until they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Uses everything shared with them, and can share folders they own. |
| **Manager** | Everything a member can do, plus inviting people. |
| **Admin** | Full control, including sharing on every folder in the team. Only admins can invite another admin. |

The invitations you've sent sit under **Invited** at the bottom of the People list, each with its status: **Waiting**, **Declined** or **Expired**. **Send again** sends a fresh email. **Withdraw** cancels one that is still waiting. An invitation to join as an admin can be sent again or withdrawn only by an admin.

## Accept an invitation

Open the link in the invitation email and sign in with the address it was sent to. Click **Accept and join**, or **Decline**. It works on a phone too.

## What the Team page shows

- **The team cover and name.** Admins see a **Change cover** button on the cover. It opens a window with ready-made covers, **Upload image** and **Remove cover**. If you belong to several teams, click the team name to switch.
- **People**: everyone in the team, with their photo, name and role, and the invitations you've sent. The photo is the one each person sets in [Settings → Account](/settings/account); until then, the first letter of their name stands in.
- **Shared folders**, split into **Shared by you** and **Shared with you**: each folder with what it holds and who has it (**Whole team**, or the photos or initials of the people). Open **Manage sharing** on your own folders, or **View access** on the others. To read the snippets and prompts inside a folder, open it on the Snippets or Prompts page.
