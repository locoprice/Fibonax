An AI app such as Claude or ChatGPT can work with your Fibonax prompts and Brains. You make a private link in Fibonax and paste it into the app once. After that, the app can look up a prompt or a Brain when you ask it to, with no copying and pasting.

## What the AI app can do

- **Find** your prompts and Brain items by words, and list them.
- **Read a prompt**, ready to use. If the prompt carries a Brain, the Brain's items come with it.
- **Read a Brain item**, or a whole Brain.
- **Save a new item to a Brain**, but only if you turned saving on for that link. It never changes or deletes anything, and what it saves is marked **AI generated** so you can check it. See [Review status](/review/overview).

It sees what you see in the dashboard: your own prompts, the prompts in team folders shared with you, and your own Brains. It never reaches your snippets. Archived items are left out, and a deprecated item comes with a note saying so.

## Make a link

1. In the dashboard, open **Settings**, then **Integrations**.
2. On the **AI apps** card, click **Connect an AI app**.
3. Choose **Claude** or **ChatGPT**, or choose **Another app** and type its name.
4. To let the app save notes to your Brains, turn on **Let it save to your Brains**. Leave it off and the app can only read.
5. Click **Create link**, then **Copy**.

<Warning>
The link works like a password: anyone who has it can read your prompts and Brains. Keep it private. Fibonax shows it only once, so if you lose it, make a new one.
</Warning>

## Add the link to your AI app

AI apps keep this in their settings, usually under **Connectors**. Some apps call them **Integrations** or **Apps**.

1. Open the AI app's settings and find **Connectors**.
2. Add a custom connector, name it Fibonax, and paste the link.
3. No sign-in is needed: the link carries everything the app needs.

Then just ask. For example: "Use my Fibonax prompt for the weekly report", or "Check my Fibonax Brain about our prices before you answer."

Apps that show prompts as commands, such as Claude, also list your Fibonax prompts there once the link is added.

## Uses

Each time the app reads the full text of a prompt, a Brain item or a whole Brain, it counts as one use, the same as copying it yourself. Searching and listing do not count. A link can make up to 60 requests a minute.

## See and switch off links

The **AI apps** card lists every link that works: the app's name, what it can do and when it was last used. Click **Switch off** to stop a link at once. To connect that app again, make a new link. You can have up to 21 links at a time.

## If something goes wrong

The AI app passes on a short message from Fibonax:

- **This Fibonax link was switched off or is not valid**: make a new link and add it to the app again.
- **This link reaches Brains only**: the link was made before prompts could be shared this way. Make a new link.
- **This link can read but not save**: make a new link with **Let it save to your Brains** turned on.
- **Too many requests in a minute**: wait a moment, then ask again.
- **Today's free uses are used up**: try again tomorrow, or add FiboTokens.
