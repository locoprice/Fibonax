A Brain is a collection of what your AI assistant should know about your work: facts, notes, documents and saved conversations. You keep it in Fibonax, and add the relevant parts to a chat in ChatGPT, Claude or another assistant when you need them.

## Create a Brain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Brains">
    In the dashboard sidebar, click **Brains**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Brain">
    Give it a name, like `Product reference`, and a line on what belongs in it. Use the words its items will use: Fibonax reads them to suggest this Brain when you save something.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Brain">
    It opens, ready for its first item.
  </Step>
</Steps>

One Brain is your default. It can't be moved to the trash.

## Find a Brain

The bar above your Brains works like the one on Prompts:

- **Contents**: show only Brains **With items**, or only **Empty** ones. The button then reads, for example, **Contents: Empty**. Pick the same value again to turn it off.
- **Updated**: show Brains changed in the **Last 7 days** or the **Last 30 days**, or the ones **Older than 30 days**.
- **Sort**: click **Name**, **Updated** or **Items**. Click the same one again to flip the order. Your default Brain always stays first.
- **Clear**: when a filter is narrowing the list, **Clear** removes them all. If nothing matches, **Clear filters** in the middle of the page does the same.
- **Atlas**: shows your Brains in orbit in place of the cards. See the next section.
- **Trash**: opens the Brains you moved to the trash.

## See your Brains in orbit

Click **Atlas** in the bar, beside **Trash**, to swap the cards for the Brain Atlas: a picture of all your Brains at once. It fills the screen, and the page scrolls so all of it is in view. Click **Atlas** again to go back to the cards. The dashboard remembers which one you left on.

- **The opening**: each time the atlas opens, the hub lights up, a wave of light runs through the brain and your Brains fly out to their orbits, closest first, their items after them. It takes about three seconds.
- **The picture**: each Brain circles the Fibonax hub like a planet. The first Brain you made stays closest. Each item circles its Brain as a small moon, and a Brain with more items is drawn larger. A Brain with more than 16 items shows a dotted ring for the rest.
- **The orbits**: they widen along a golden spiral, the shape behind the Fibonacci numbers 8, 13, 21, 34.
- **Point at something** to hold the orbits still and read its name. A Brain you point at lights up with a line to each of its items, and the other Brains fade back. Click a Brain, an item or the hub to read about it in the panel on the right.
- **The panel** lists your Brains, closest first, with how many items each holds. Click a Brain to see its description and its items, then **Open Brain** to go to it, or **Back** to return to the list.
- **Pause** stops the motion and **Play** starts it again. If your computer is set to reduce motion, the atlas opens already in place and starts paused.

The filters narrow the atlas the same way they narrow the cards. **Sort** is put away while the atlas is on: the orbits always run from your oldest Brain outwards.

The atlas holds up to 13 Brains at once. With more, it shows the 13 you updated last, and says so.

## Fill a Brain

- **Add text**: write a fact, a note or a conversation. Give it a name, a one-line **Summary** and the **Body**. Item names are in capital letters: whatever you type or paste turns into capitals. The editor shows how long it is in tokens, the unit AI assistants measure text in. **Draft from text** at the top of the editor names and summarises text you paste. See [Draft with AI](/draft/overview).
- **Upload file**: PDF, Word (`.docx`), text (`.txt`, `.md`) or CSV files up to 20 MB. Fibonax reads the text and splits it into pieces your assistant can use.
- **Always attached**: switch this on for items that should go into every context you add, like your house style.

Keep each fact to a single idea. Short, separate items fit into a chat more easily than one long one.

Each item keeps its earlier versions: open it and click **History** to see them and restore one.

## Review status

Each item has a review status, like snippets and prompts. Items an AI assistant saves into your Brains are marked **AI generated** until you approve them. A deprecated item still works, and the Context button marks it before you add it to a chat. An archived item is never added to a chat. See [Review and approval](/review/overview).

## Use a Brain in an AI chat

To let an AI app look things up in your Brains by itself, without the extension, give it a private link. See [Connect an AI app](/integrations/ai-apps).

On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, DeepSeek and Copilot, the extension adds a small Fibonax **T** just above the message box. It shows only next to the chat, and steps aside while a window such as the site's Settings covers it.

1. Start writing your message.
2. Click the **T**. It opens into **Context** with the list. Or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> (<kbd>Cmd</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>M</kbd> on a Mac).
3. Fibonax picks the items that match what you're writing and puts them above your message as normal text.

The list shows items from your Brains and your [prompts](/prompts/overview), never your snippets. A tag on each row says which it is: **Brain** or **Prompt**. You see exactly what will be sent, and you can edit or delete any of it before you send.

### Choose what goes in

Three buttons at the top of the list decide what it shows and adds:

- **Prompts**: your prompts only.
- **Brains**: items from your Brains only.
- **Both**: the two together. This is how it starts. When you add both kinds, the prompts go in first and the Brain items follow.

Your choice is remembered on that computer, for every chat site.

### Search the list

Type in the search box under the three buttons to find a prompt or a Brain item by name, whatever your message says. The list updates as you type. Tick what you want, then search again for the next one: what you ticked stays ticked at the top of the list. Click **Insert** when you have everything.

Empty the box, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to go back to the items that match your message. **Refresh** reads your message again.

### Usually added together

With **Both** chosen, Fibonax notices which Brain items you add together with a prompt. Once you have added the same items with a prompt at least twice, and at least half the times you used it, they tick themselves when you tick that prompt. Each one says why, for example **Usually with Customer reply**.

- A prompt brings up to three items. One that would not fit the token budget is listed but not ticked.
- Untick any of them before you click **Insert**. Leave an item out often enough and it stops being suggested.
- Untick the prompt and the items it brought are unticked with it.

Fibonax keeps only a count of which items went in together, on that computer. It is not sent anywhere, so each computer learns your habits on its own.

### From the extension popup

Click the Fibonax icon in the toolbar, open **Brains** and click a Brain. **Copy whole Brain** copies everything in it, in the same form the Context button adds. Paste it into the AI chat at the start of your message. To copy one item, click it. A search on the list of Brains also looks inside every Brain. It reads each item's name, summary and text, needs every word you type, in any order, and shows the sentence a word was found in.

### On your phone

On the [phone page](/apps/mobile#brains), tap **Brains**, open a Brain and tap **Copy whole Brain**. Paste it into the AI app at the start of your message. You can also copy or share a single item.

### In a prompt

Give a prompt a Brain in its Context block, and the Brain goes in with the prompt every time you use it: from the `"""` menu, with its shortcut, from the Context button, from the popup, from the dashboard and on your phone. The Brain is read each time, so the prompt always carries it as it is that day. See [Write a prompt](/prompts/create-prompts#use-a-brain-as-context).

## Save things into a Brain

- **Selected text on any page**: highlight it, right-click and choose **Save selection to Fibonax**. Pick a Brain and click **Save**.
- **A whole AI conversation**: on a ChatGPT or Claude conversation, open the Fibonax popup. A **Save this chat** card appears. Pick a Brain and click **Save**.
- **From your phone**: share text or a link from any app to Fibonax, or copy it and tap **Paste & save**. See [Save from other apps](/apps/mobile#save-from-other-apps). To write an item yourself, open a Brain on the [phone page](/apps/mobile#brains) and tap **Add text**.

Each of these suggests the Brain that fits best: the one whose name, description and items share the most words with what you're saving. A line under the Brain list says why, or that no Brain fits yet, in which case the Brain you used last stays selected. You can always pick another. A Brain's description helps here: list the words its items use.

## Keep your AI's memory small

AI apps such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini keep their own memory about you. Its top layer has to stay small: when it grows too big, the AI has too much to remember and starts to lose track. Clean it once in a while.

Under your Brains, on the dashboard and on the phone page, open **Keep your AI's memory small** and click **Copy prompt**. Paste it into the AI you use and answer its questions. It asks the AI to:

1. List every memory, one line each.
2. Mark each one to keep, fix or delete.
3. Ask you about anything it's not sure of.
4. Show you the cleaned version, under 30 lines, and tell you where to click to change it.

## Trash

Items, files and whole Brains go to the trash first. Open **Trash** to restore them, or delete them for good. Deleting a Brain for good removes everything in it, files included.

<Note>
  You manage Brains in the dashboard. The phone page reads, copies and adds to them, and edits an item's name and text; creating, renaming and deleting Brains, moving or deleting items and uploading files happen on a computer.
</Note>
