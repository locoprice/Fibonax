Fibonax has one search window. It looks through your snippets, prompts and Brains together, and it works the same way on the dashboard, in the extension popup and on your phone.

## Open it

- **Dashboard:** click the search bar at the top, or press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) on any page.
- **Extension popup:** click the search bar, press **Ctrl+K**, or start typing. The window opens with your first letter in it.
- **Phone:** tap the search box on the Snippets, Prompts or Brains page, or tap **More**, then **Search this section**. The window takes the whole screen, and **Close** puts it away.

The search bar opens the window and takes no text itself. To narrow the list on a page, use that page's filters.

## Before you type

With nothing typed, the window lists what you opened lately, newest first, under **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Past week**, **Past 30 days** and **Older**. It keeps your last 50 items.

The list stays on the device you're using, so your computer and your phone each have their own. To empty it, click the gear at the bottom of the window, then **Clear recently opened**.

## Type to search

Results update as you type.

- Every word you type has to be somewhere in the item: its name, its trigger or shortcut, its search keywords, its labels, the folder or Brain it sits in, or its text. The order of the words doesn't matter.
- Capitals and accents don't matter: **cafe** finds "Café".
- Items whose name or shortcut holds your words come first. Items found through their text come after, with the sentence the word was found in.
- Each row shows the item's name and where it sits, for example **Snippets / Billing** or **Brains / Company policies**.

When nothing matches, the window says **No results**. If a filter is on, **Clear filters** turns them all off.

## Narrow the results

The chips under the search box filter the list. A chip that is on is tinted and has a cross to clear it.

| Chip | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title only** | Reads names and shortcuts only, not the text. |
| **Created by** | Shows what the people you pick created. It appears when your library holds items from more than one person. |
| **In** | Shows what sits in the folders or Brains you pick. |
| **+ Filter** | Adds **Type** (Snippets, Prompts or Brains), **Labels**, or **Date edited** (Today, Past week, Past 30 days or Older). |

Filters start clean each time you open the window. On your phone, a teammate shows as **Teammate** under **Created by**.

## Keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **↑** and **↓** | Move the highlight. The mouse moves the same highlight. |
| **Enter** | Opens the highlighted item. |
| **Ctrl+Enter** (**⌘Enter** on a Mac) | On the dashboard, opens the item in a new tab. In the popup, opens it on the dashboard. |
| **Esc** | Clears what you typed. Press it again to close the window. |

A click outside the window closes it too.

## What opening an item does

| Item | Dashboard | Extension popup | Phone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Snippet | Opens it in the editor | Shows it with its fill form, ready to copy | Opens it |
| Prompt | Opens it in the editor | Copies it | Copies it |
| Brain | Opens the Brain | Opens the Brain | Opens the Brain |
| Brain item | Opens it in its Brain | Copies its text | Opens it |

## Ask FiboChat

On the dashboard and your phone, the sparkle button beside the search box switches the window from searching to asking. Type a question and press **Enter**, or click **Ask**. Click the sparkle again to go back to searching. See [FiboChat](/ask/overview).
