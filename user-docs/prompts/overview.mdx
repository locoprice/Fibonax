A prompt is a longer instruction for an AI assistant: who it should be, what it should do, and the rules it should follow. Fibonax keeps your prompts next to your snippets, in the same folders, so your team works from the same wording.

## Insert a prompt

- **From the menu.** Type `"""` in any text field. The **Prompt mode** menu opens with your own prompts first, then the [built-in prompts](#built-in-prompts) behind the last row. Keep typing to narrow the list, then press <kbd>Tab</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- **With its shortcut.** Give a prompt a shortcut, like `followup`. Typing `"""followup` inserts it straight away, without the menu.
- **With the Context button.** On ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and the other AI chats, **Context** lists the prompts that match what you're writing, next to your Brains. Choose **Prompts** at the top to see prompts only, and use the search box to find one by name. See [Brains](/brains/overview#choose-what-goes-in).
- **By copying it.** On the Prompts page, click **Use** on a prompt card to open it, then **Copy**. On your phone, use **Copy text** or **Use now**.

A prompt goes in as plain text. Unlike a snippet, it has no fill form.

A prompt you saved a moment ago is in the menu as soon as you type `"""`. You don't need to open the Fibonax popup first.

A prompt that uses a [Brain](/prompts/create-prompts#use-a-brain-as-context) goes in with everything in that Brain, as the Brain is at that moment, whichever way you insert or copy it. From the `"""` menu, the menu says **Adding your Brain…** for a moment while it reads the Brain. When a Brain cannot be added, a line in the prompt says so, and so does the message after copying.

`"""` is the default prompt trigger. You can change it in [Triggers](/settings/triggers).

## Built-in prompts

The `"""` menu carries seven prompts that come with Fibonax. They sit behind its last row, **Built-in prompts**: pick that row to open them, or type a word of the name and the menu finds it. Until you save a prompt of your own, they're listed directly.

| Prompt | What it asks the AI for |
|---|---|
| **Summarize** | The main point, the key points, and what is still open. |
| **Change the tone** | Your text in the tone you name, saying the same thing. |
| **Translate** | Your text in the language you name, with the same tone, names and numbers. |
| **Review and improve** | What gets in the reader's way, then a better version. |
| **Think it through** | The options for a question or a problem, weighed, and a recommendation. |
| **Plan the steps** | A goal turned into steps in order, with the main risk. |
| **Check the facts** | The numbers, dates, names and claims that look wrong, and what to verify yourself. |

A built-in prompt goes in ready to use: type or paste your text under it and send. When your message leaves out something the AI needs, such as the language to translate into, the AI asks you before it answers. Each prompt also has the AI check its own answer before it replies.

A prompt of your own with the same name as a built-in one takes its place in the menu. With [Interactive Steps](/prompts/create-prompts#interactive-steps) switched on in the popup, built-in prompts start with the step-by-step instructions too.
