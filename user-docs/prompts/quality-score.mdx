The prompt editor scores the prompt out of 10 while you write. The **Quality** row shows the score and how many tips are waiting:

- **8 or more**: Excellent.
- **5 to 7.9**: Good.
- **Below 5**: Needs improvement.

Click the row to see each check, why it passed or not, and an example of what would pass.

## What it checks

| Check | Passes when |
|---|---|
| Who the AI is | **Who the AI is** is added and names a role. |
| Clear task | **What should the AI do?** states the task. It counts the most. |
| Background given | **Background** has what the AI needs to know, or a Brain. |
| Rules set | **Rules** sets limits, like length or tone. |
| Example included | **An example** shows what a good answer looks like. |
| Audience named | The prompt says who the answer is for. |
| Self-check asked | The prompt asks the AI to check its work or flag doubts. |
| Answer format set | An answer format is chosen in More options. |
| Strategy chosen | A strategy is chosen in More options. |
| Enough detail | The whole prompt is detailed enough to be clear. |
| AI model chosen | A preferred AI model is set. |

A check can pass in part, for example a part that's added but thin.

## Fix it in one click

A check that doesn't pass shows a button with the fix, such as **Add rules**, **Name the audience**, **Add self-check** or **Apply One-shot**. Click it and Fibonax makes the change for you.

## Compare with your library

When you have enough prompts, the score also says how this one compares, for example "Stronger than 68% of your 12 prompts".

<Note>
  The score measures structure, not results. A prompt you've tested on real work may beat a higher-scoring one.
</Note>
