Click **Refer a friend** near the bottom of the dashboard sidebar.

## How it works

1. Share your unique private link with up to 3 friends.
2. Your friend opens an account through the link and subscribes to Pro within 7 days.
3. You both get a free month of Pro.

The free month is added to your Pro time by itself. If you are not on Pro, it starts that day. If you are, it is added after the time you already paid for.

## Share your link

Your link is under **Your invite link**. Click **Copy** and paste it into an email or a chat. The link is yours alone: anyone who opens an account through it is counted as your friend.

## Friends who joined

The window lists each friend who opened an account through your link, with part of their email address hidden, and where they stand:

- **Days left to subscribe**: your friend still has time.
- **Subscribed. You both got a free month.**
- **Did not subscribe in time.**: the 7 days passed. This friend no longer takes one of your 3 places, so you can invite someone else.

You can earn 3 free months at most.

## If a friend invited you

Open your account through your friend's link and the sign-up page says so. Subscribe to Pro within 7 days of opening your account and you both get a free month.

If you opened your account without the link, open **Refer a friend**, click **Did a friend invite you?**, paste the link or the code and click **Add**. This works during the first 7 days after you opened your account, and only before you subscribe.

## Limits

- 3 friends per person.
- 7 days to subscribe, counted from the day the friend opened their account.
- One friend's link per account. It cannot be changed afterwards.
- You cannot use your own link.
