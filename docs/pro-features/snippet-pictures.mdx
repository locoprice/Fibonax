## Pictures

A logo, a map or a photo, sent with the text. Open **Pictures** in the panel on the left of the editor and click **Add picture**:

1. **Choose the picture**: drop it in, or choose a file. PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP, up to 5 MB. Large photos are made smaller for you.
2. **Say what it shows**, like `Company logo`. People who cannot see pictures hear this, and it shows where the picture does not load.
3. **Size**: Small, Medium or Full width.

Click **Insert picture**. The picture goes in at the cursor.

Where the snippet lands decides what goes in:

- **Emails** (Gmail, Outlook on the web) get the text and the picture.
- **Chats and plain text boxes** (WhatsApp, most forms) get the text only. The fill form says so before you insert.
- **Copy**, in the popup, the editor's preview and the phone, copies both: paste into an email and the picture goes in too.
- **Share** on the phone sends the text only.

Pictures already in the snippet are listed under the button, with **Edit** and **Remove**. A picture you remove stays online, so emails you already sent keep showing it.
