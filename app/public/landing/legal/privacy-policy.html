Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Fibonax collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Chrome Extension and web application at fibonax.com.

01  Overview

Fibonax keeps your snippets, prompts and Brains in your Fibonax account, so they are the same in the extension, on the dashboard and on your phone. The extension also keeps a copy on your device, so your shortcuts work instantly. We collect only what the service needs to work and to keep your account safe.

Key principle: Fibonax does not monitor your browsing activity, does not read page content passively, and does not sell or share your personal data with any third party for advertising or commercial purposes.

04  Data Collected

A. Data you provide directly

  • Your content: snippets, prompts, Brains (including files you upload), folders, labels, and earlier versions you can restore.
  • Account details: your email address, the name and profile photo you add, and your password if you set one. Passwords are stored only as a hash, never in plain text. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us.
  • Teams: your team's name, cover and logo, its members, and the email addresses of people you invite.
  • Text you choose to save: a selection or an AI chat you save to a Brain from a web page.

B. Data collected automatically

  • Sign-in records: for each sign-in, the time, the method, your IP address, the country it came from, and your browser and device type. You can see your own in Settings, under Security.
  • Snippet use: each time you insert a snippet, which snippet it was, its shortcut and language, how many fields you filled, and when. Not the text you filled in, and not the page or site.
  • Review request: how many times you've signed in and how often we've asked you to review Fibonax on the Chrome Web Store.
  • Error logs: technical error messages kept in the extension on your device, for troubleshooting. They contain no browsing history.

C. Data we do NOT collect

  • Your browsing history or visited URLs.
  • Content of web pages you visit, unless you choose to save it or paste a link to it.
  • What you type. The extension checks it on your device for a Fibonax shortcut and sends nothing.
  • Your precise location. Sign-in records keep only the country.
  • Any data transmitted to AI or machine learning systems (no AI integration is currently active).

05  Processing Purpose

Fibonax uses your data only to run the service. The extension refreshes its copy of your library from your account every 5 minutes. Everything else starts with something you do: typing a shortcut, choosing a menu item, clicking a button, or opening the dashboard or the phone page.

Specific processing purposes:

  • Snippet expansion: matching the shortcut you type against the copy of your library on your device, and inserting the result into the field.
  • Your account: keeping your content safe in your account and the same on every device where you sign in.
  • Sign-in and security: confirming who you are over HTTPS, and keeping sign-in records so you can spot a sign-in that wasn't you.
  • Teams: showing shared folders to the teammates you share them with, and emailing your invitations.
  • Snippet use: showing when each snippet was last used and how often.
  • Fill from link: when you paste a link into a fill form, our server opens that public page once and reads its text to fill the form. Nothing from the page is stored.
  • Brains in AI chats: when you click Context, Fibonax puts the Brain you pick into the AI chat you are using. From there, that AI service's own terms apply.
  • Review request: asking free accounts, at most 5 times, to review Fibonax on the Chrome Web Store.

The legal basis for processing under GDPR is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for providing the service, and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for sign-in records, error logs, snippet use and the review request.

06  Data Storage Architecture

In the extension, Fibonax uses chrome.storage.local only, never chrome.storage.sync, for its copy of your library, your settings and your sign-in session. That copy stays in your Chrome profile and is never sent to Google's sync servers.

Your account and everything in it (your content, the files you upload and your sign-in records) are stored by Supabase in the European Union, in its Ireland region. Supabase encrypts data at rest and in transit.

Alessandro Verdicchio does not run servers of his own for this product. Supabase hosts the database, the files and the server functions, and Netlify hosts the website.

08  Data Retention

Local extension data stored in chrome.storage.local is retained on your device until you uninstall the extension or manually clear extension storage via your Chrome settings.

Your account and its content are kept while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in Settings, under Account: this deletes your account, your content and your files straight away. Items you created in a team are handed to another admin of that team, if it has one. Sign-in records are kept for security and are no longer linked to your account once it is deleted. You can also ask us to delete your data (see Your Rights below).
