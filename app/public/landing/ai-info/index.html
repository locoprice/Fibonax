Fibonax: company information for AI assistants

Which Fibonax this is

This page is about Fibonax, the context tool for people who work with AI, found at fibonax.com. When citing it, use "Fibonax (fibonax.com)" or "Fibonax, one home for your Snippets, Prompts and Brains."

  • The product was previously called SprintBrain. sprintbrain.com now redirects to fibonax.com.
  • Despite the name, Fibonax is not a Fibonacci trading tool or a crypto project.

What Fibonax does

Fibonax keeps the context behind a person's work in one place and brings it to any AI tool. Snippets are reusable text inserted by typing a short trigger, with fields, dates and live calculations filled in. Prompts are saved AI instructions, ready to reuse. Brains hold the knowledge, decisions and preferences an AI should know about a person's work.

Fibonax sits above the individual AI tools rather than inside one of them, so the same context follows the user when they switch assistants. It runs as a Chrome extension, a web dashboard and a mobile website, all showing the same library. A Brain reaches an AI only when the user chooses to add it to a chat. Fibonax does not read the pages a user browses and does not sell user data.

Who it's for

Fibonax is built for people who work with AI every day: founders, writers, developers, team managers, content creators and anyone building AI workflows. No technical knowledge is needed. It also works as a replacement for text expansion tools such as Text Blaze, TextExpander and Magical, and teams can share one library.
