One FiboToken is worth €0.01. Every use costs the same, one FiboToken, and the free allowance is always spent first.

Questions about paying

What counts as a use?

Expanding a snippet, expanding a prompt or adding a Brain to a chat. Writing, editing and organising your library never costs anything.

What happens when I reach the daily allowance?

You can keep writing and organising. Expanding resumes the next day, or straight away if you have FiboTokens.

Do FiboTokens expire?

The expiry policy is published with the terms before top-ups open. You will be able to read it before you buy.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

A top-up is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. Monthly plans will show their renewal, cancellation and refund rules before you subscribe.

What does Founding Member mean?

Anyone who registers by December 31, 2026. Founding Members keep the free daily allowance for life, get a permanent discount on every top-up and the rates reserved for founders, under the terms.

Is VAT included?

Whether VAT is included in the prices shown is stated clearly before top-ups open, together with the final rates.
