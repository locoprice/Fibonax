Questions about paying

Is Pay as you go a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. You pay once, the FiboTokens land in your account and they stay there until you use them. Nothing renews.

Do FiboTokens expire?

No. FiboTokens you buy never expire. They stay in your account until you use them.

Can the exchange rate change?

Yes. Like a currency, the FiboToken rate is variable and can move with the market. Any change applies to new packs only and is shown on this page before you buy. FiboTokens you already own keep their value: one FiboToken, one use.

What counts as a use?

Expanding a snippet, expanding a prompt or adding a Brain to a chat. Writing, editing and organising your library never costs anything.

What happens when I reach the daily allowance?

You can keep writing and organising. Expanding resumes the next day, or straight away with FiboTokens or the monthly plan.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

A pack is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. The monthly plan shows its renewal, cancellation and refund rules before you subscribe.

What does Founding Member mean?

Anyone who registers by December 31, 2026. Founding Members keep the free daily allowance for life, get 20% off every FiboToken pack on top of the pack bonus, and the rates reserved for founders, under the terms.

Is VAT included?

Yes. Every price on this page includes VAT. What you see is what you pay.
